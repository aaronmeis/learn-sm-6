Corrections made in narration

  • Slide 1: Mk 72 is the booster; the slide reuses it for the dual-thrust motor. Range rings are decorative, not range data.
  • Slide 3: 'Propulsion' is labeled twice; it is one booster and one dual-thrust motor.
  • Slide 5: Link 16 appears under Share but is not in the pack's sources.
  • Slide 6: 'Proven' on air warfare and terminal BMD is read as demonstrated in test; DOT&E has not established operational effectiveness.
  • Slide 7: SM-2 is a medium-to-long-range area-defense missile, not close-in; RAM is not in the pack's sources.
  • Slide 11: 'Lack of at-sea reload' overstates it; the sources say reloading at sea is constrained.
  • Slide 13: the $2K drone and $2B destroyer figures are illustrations, not sourced.
