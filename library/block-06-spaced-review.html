Day blocks

Block 6: Spaced review

Time: 20 minutes Goal: Retrieval without re-reading Pack: Raytheon SM-6 (sm-6)

Focus from mind map

  • Identity, missions, kill chain, evidence record, variants, economics.

Sources

  • quiz-bank (answer closed-book)
  • cheatsheet (only after grading)

Actions

  1. 12m. Close all notes. Answer quiz-bank items 1 to 15 from memory.
  2. 5m. Grade yourself. Restudy only the misses, using the block that covers each one.
  3. 3m. Put two micro-reviews on the calendar: 24 hours after the study day (quiz misses only) and 7 days after (full quiz, 10 minutes).

Retrieval check

Question

From memory: three facts and one process you must not forget about Raytheon SM-6.

Answer cues

Facts: IOC November 2013. FTM-32 (March 2024) was a live terminal intercept of an advanced MRBM target with SM-6 Dual II SWUP from an Aegis Baseline 9 ship. FTX-40 (March 2025) was a simulated engagement of a hypersonic-representative target, with no missile fired. Process: detect, share track, evaluate and assign, launch, update in flight, seeker terminal homing, kill assessment.

Exit criteria

  • Quiz attempted closed-book
  • Misses restudied
  • 24h and 7d reviews scheduled
