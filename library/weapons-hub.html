Business Explanation

Business Explanation

This section collects open-source study of weapon systems, one system at a time, so the learner can talk about them accurately with defense program people and spot overclaims in briefs, vendor material, and press. Each system gets one assessment page and, when it earns a study day, a one-day mastery pack. The first system is the Raytheon SM-6 missile family.

TL;DR

One folder per system under Learning/Weapons/<System>/. The system's assessment page is the spine and feeds a /one-day-mastery pack as its Mode A mind map. SM-6 is first: assessment page → one-day pack. Grade every claim with the evidence ladder.

Scope and guardrails

  • Sources: open and unclassified only. Prefer program offices (Navy, Army, MDA), DOT&E, CRS, GAO, and CSIS. Label vendor material as vendor.
  • In scope: missions, architecture and kill chain, variants, test evidence, acquisition, production, and cost.
  • Out of scope: classified parameters, targeting, employment procedures, and techniques for defeating a fielded system. Countermeasures stay at the level of broad classes, as on the SM-6 page.
  • Numbers: a range or performance figure without an official source is an estimate. Say so or leave it out. Give every cost its basis and year.

Adding a system

  1. Create Learning/Weapons/<System>/ and file the assessment page there with frontmatter (type: brief, topic: weapons), a Location line, and a Business Explanation callout.
  2. Run /one-day-mastery in Mode A with that page as the mind map.
  3. Add a row to the catalog above.
