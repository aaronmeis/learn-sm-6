Business Explanation

A one-day study plan for the Raytheon SM-6 missile family, built from the first page of the Weapons section. By the end of the day the learner can explain what SM-6 does, why its reach depends on the sensor network around it, and which public claims the test record supports. The plan stays on open sources and leaves classified performance blank.
