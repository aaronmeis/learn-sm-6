GLOSSARY — Raytheon SM-6

Terms earned after demonstrated understanding.

Add terms only after the learner can use them correctly. Seeded from the spine page. Strike any row you cannot yet use.

Term Definition Block
RIM-174 ERAM Formal name of SM-6: Extended Range Active Missile. 1
Active radar homing The missile's own radar finds and tracks the target in the terminal phase. 1
Semi-active radar homing The missile homes on energy the ship's illuminator reflects off the target. 1
Dual-mode seeker SM-6 seeker that supports active and semi-active modes. Mode logic is not public. 1
Blast-fragmentation Proximity warhead that damages the target with fragments. Used by SM-6. 1
Hit-to-kill Destroys the target by direct collision. Used by SM-3 and PAC-3. 1, 2
Endo-atmospheric Inside the atmosphere. SM-6 terminal BMD happens here. 1, 2
Exo-atmospheric Outside the atmosphere. SM-3 midcourse intercepts happen here. 1, 2
Terminal phase Last part of a ballistic missile's flight, as it descends toward the target. 1
Sea-Based Terminal (SBT) The program that gave SM-6 its terminal BMD role (Dual I, Dual II). 1, 5
Aegis Baseline 9 Aegis combat-system software baseline that supports NIFC-CA and integrated air and missile defense. 1
SPY-6 Newer Navy radar family. More sensitive than SPY-1, built to support Standard Missile variants. 1
CEC Cooperative Engagement Capability. Shares sensor data so units hold one composite fire-control track. 1, 2
NIFC-CA Naval Integrated Fire Control–Counter Air. The engage-on-remote kill chain using E-2D, CEC, Aegis, and SM-6. 1, 2
Launch-on-remote Fire using another sensor's track, then guide with your own sensor. 1
Engage-on-remote Another sensor supports the engagement through to intercept. 1
Mk 41 VLS Navy vertical launching system. One SM-6 occupies one cell. 1, 2
Typhon (MRC) Army Mid-Range Capability. Containerized launcher that fires SM-6 and Tomahawk. 1
IBCS / LTAMDS Army battle command system and lower-tier radar. Linked to SM-6 in a 2024 simulation. 1, 5
AIM-174B Gunslinger Air-launched SM-6 configuration for the F/A-18E/F. Range classified. 1
Block IB Planned larger-motor SM-6 for more range and speed. Put on strategic pause in 2025. 2, 5
SWUP Software upgrade. Paired with Dual II for the FTM-32 intercept. 1, 3
DOT&E Director, Operational Test and Evaluation. Independent judge of operational effectiveness. 2, 5
FTM-32 March 2024 live intercept of an advanced MRBM target in terminal phase. 3, 5
FTX-40 March 2025 tracking and simulated engagement of a hypersonic-representative target. No missile fired. 2, 5
GPI Glide Phase Interceptor. Separate program to engage hypersonic weapons earlier than SM-6 can. 2
Cost-exchange ratio Interceptor cost divided by threat cost. Ignores the value of what is defended. 2, 5
