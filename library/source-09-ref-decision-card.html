One-card method for grading any public SM-6 claim against the evidence ladder.

Use this card in block 3 (drill B), block 5, and block 7.

The evidence ladder (weakest to strongest)

Rung Example What it can support
1. Vendor or press claim "Three missiles in one" A stated intent or marketing position
2. Concept animation MDA GPI scenario animation (2021) How a program says layers fit together
3. Simulated engagement FTX-40 (2025), Valiant Shield (2024) Sensor, C2, and software chain works in simulation
4. Developmental or MDA live intercept 2016 NIFC-CA, FTM-31 E1a (2023), FTM-32 (2024) The weapon can do it under test conditions
5. Exercise against a real target Talisman Sabre sinking (2025) Works outside a scripted test, still not against a defended peer
6. DOT&E operational test Not completed for recent SM-6 BMD capability (FY2024 report) Operational effectiveness, suitability, survivability
7. Sustained combat record Red Sea, Operation Epic Fury (public counts are combined or estimated) Real use, but rarely with controlled data

Five questions for any claim

  1. Which rung is the evidence on?
  2. Which kill-chain link was assumed (sensor, network, decision, launcher, seeker)?
  3. Was a classified number stated as fact?
  4. Is a cost or quantity given with its basis and year?
  5. What would change the answer, and when will we know?

Signals to watch (known-by)

Signal Why it matters
FTM-43 live hypersonic-representative intercept Moves counter-hypersonic from rung 3 to rung 4
Block IB restart or cancellation Settles the kinematic upgrade path
Next DOT&E annual report Only source for rung 6
FY2027 SM-6 appropriation (540 requested) Turns the production objective into funded rounds
