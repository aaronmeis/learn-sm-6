Day blocks

Block 5: Edge cases

Time: 45 minutes Goal: Exceptions, traps, failure modes Pack: Raytheon SM-6 (sm-6)

Focus from mind map

Most SM-6 mistakes are about evidence and numbers, not engineering. Work the trap table below.

Sources

  • SM-6 assessment page, sections "Risks and unknowns", "Evidence and test record", "Deployment and industrial base"
  • DOT&E FY2024 SM-6
  • DOT&E FY2022 SM-6
  • MDA FTX-40 release
  • Naval News, Block IB strategic pause
  • CSIS, Rebuilding U.S. Missile Inventory (May 2026)

Actions

  1. 15m. Read the two DOT&E reports (two pages each) and "Risks and unknowns".
  2. 20m. Cover the right two columns of the table. For each symptom, say the cause and the fix aloud, then check.
  3. 10m. Add your top three traps to cheatsheet.
# Symptom Cause Fix
1 A source states a precise SM-6 range Range is classified. Explainer videos repeat unofficial estimates. Say "classified; open-source estimates vary" and cite no number as fact.
2 "SM-6 is proven against hypersonics" FTX-40 was tracking plus a simulated engagement. FTM-43 not publicly flown. Place the claim on the evidence ladder. Say "limited terminal defense, live intercept pending".
3 "Combat-proven" used as "operationally effective" Combat use and DOT&E operational test answer different questions. Cite DOT&E FY2024: FTM-32 was not designed to determine effectiveness, lethality, suitability, or survivability.
4 Block IB "arrives FY2027" DOT&E FY2022 date, superseded by the FY2026 budget pause. Treat Block IB as developmental. Watch for a restart decision.
5 Unit cost quoted as $2.97M, $4.3M, or higher Constant FY2004 APUC, then-year budget cost, and other bases differ. State the basis and year with every number.
6 "$24.4B for 500 a year" read as funded buys Framework ceiling and production objective are not appropriations. Pair it with the FY2027 request (540) and the 36 to 39 month lead time.
7 Talisman Sabre sinking read as "kills warships" A sinking exercise target is not a defended, maneuvering peer combatant. Say "demonstrated strike, effect against defended ships not public".
8 "One missile replaces SM-2, SM-3, ESSM" Multi-mission does not mean optimal for every layer. One round uses one cell. Use the layer model. SM-3 owns midcourse. ESSM gives magazine density.
9 Intact missile assumed to mean intact engagement Over-the-horizon shots depend on remote track, network, identity, authorization. Ask which link of Sense, Share, Decide, Act is assumed.
10 Contested-spectrum or cyber resilience asserted either way DOT&E found insufficient public data. Classified data may exist. Say "uncertain on the public record", not "fails" or "immune".
11 Variant names mixed up Air-warfare blocks, BMD configurations, and software upgrades overlap. Read the variant roadmap as a family tree with two upgrade axes.
12 SM-6-only combat expenditure quoted Public Red Sea and Epic Fury figures combine SM-2 and SM-6 or are estimates. Name the source and say "combined" or "estimated".

Retrieval check

Question

What edge case would break a naive approach to Raytheon SM-6?

Answer cues

Treating a demonstration as operational effectiveness (traps 2, 3, 7). Quoting a classified number as fact (trap 1). Mixing cost bases (trap 5). Each one makes a brief look confident and be wrong.

Exit criteria

  • Twelve traps worked closed-book
  • Top three traps on the cheatsheet
