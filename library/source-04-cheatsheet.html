Decision rules and tells only. Not a glossary dump.

Rules

  • When a claim is about reach, ask which sensor and network carried the track, because SM-6's over-the-horizon reach belongs to the kill chain, not the missile alone.
  • When a claim is about hypersonic defense, say "limited terminal layer, live intercept not public as of Oct 2026", because FTX-40 was simulated.
  • When a claim says proven or combat-proven, ask "proven by which event, and did DOT&E assess it?", because DOT&E says FTM-32 did not establish operational effectiveness.
  • When you see a range number, drop it, because official range is classified and explainer figures are estimates.
  • When you see a unit cost, state its basis and year, because constant-FY2004 APUC and then-year cost differ by more than a third.
  • When you see a contract ceiling or production objective, pair it with the appropriation, because a ceiling is not a funded buy.
  • When someone proposes SM-6 for a low-end threat, ask what cheaper layer covers it, because one round uses one cell.
  • When a roadmap item changes the motor, expect slower schedule and more test risk than a software change.

Tells of an overclaim

  • "Invulnerable", "unstoppable", "proven against all"
  • A precise range or Mach number with no official source
  • FTX-40 described as an intercept
  • Army "SM-6 air defense battery" cited from the Valiant Shield simulation
