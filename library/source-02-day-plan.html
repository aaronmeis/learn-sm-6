Timed schedule for the seven pedagogy blocks.

Focus total: ~350 minutes (~5.8h) plus breaks.

Times are relative from day start. Plug in wall-clock if you want.

Spine: SM-6 assessment page. Read it in pieces as each block directs, not front to back.

Order Block Minutes Relative window SM-6 focus Exit
1 Core concepts 60 +0m → +60m Identity, missions, kill-chain vocabulary 15+ terms, 3 weak marked
2 Mental models 45 +60m → +105m Kill web, layers, evidence ladder, upgrade axes, magazine economics 5 models with failure modes
3 Active practice 90 +105m → +195m Kill chain from memory, grade 10 claims, variant tree, layer table Drill A 18/21
— Break 15–20
4 Teach it 30 +195m → +225m Five-minute and two-minute teach-back Gaps fixed
5 Edge cases 45 +225m → +270m Twelve evidence and number traps Top 3 on cheatsheet
— Break 10
6 Spaced review 20 +270m → +290m Closed-book quiz 24h and 7d scheduled
7 Application 60 +290m → +350m Evidence-graded brief or EA view Artifact + learning record

Rules for the day

  • Retrieval before re-reading (blocks 4 and 6 especially).
  • Ground claims in RESOURCES / source-ledger.
  • Open sources only. Range, envelope, seeker performance, and probability of kill are classified. Leave them blank rather than estimate.
  • End with a real artifact in block 7 + a learning-record.
