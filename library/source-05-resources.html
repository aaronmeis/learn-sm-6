Gaps

  • Range, engagement envelope, seeker performance, electronic protection, and probability of kill are classified. No public source fills these. Leave them blank.
  • No public live hypersonic intercept by SM-6 as of 2026-10-04 (FTM-43 not publicly flown).
  • No SM-6-only expenditure count for the Red Sea or Operation Epic Fury. Public figures combine SM-2 and SM-6.
  • Block IB restart after the 2025 pause: no public decision found.

Rejected / low trust

  • YouTube explainer channels (Defense Updates, DIB Dispatch, and similar) that quote a precise range ("460 km", or "300 nm" for AIM-174B). These are unofficial estimates. Use them only as examples of the classified-range trap in block 5.
  • Any source that presents FTX-40 as a live hypersonic intercept.
