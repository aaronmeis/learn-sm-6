Day blocks

Block 7: Application

Time: 60 minutes Goal: Apply to a real task today Pack: Raytheon SM-6 (sm-6)

Focus from mind map

Produce one durable artifact that a decision-maker or architect could use. Pick one option.

Sources

  • SM-6 assessment page, sections "Enterprise-architecture view", "Bottom line", "Evidence and test record"
  • decision-card
  • CSIS, MDA and the 2025 Budget with LTG Heath Collins (video)
  • CSIS, Rebuilding U.S. Missile Inventory (May 2026)

Actions

Option A (default): evidence-graded brief, one page

  1. 10m. Bottom line in three sentences.
  2. 20m. A table with at least six rows: claim, status (proven, proven with limits, not public), event, source.
  3. 15m. Three investments that may matter more than missile range, each with a reason from the sources: sensors, network resilience, magazine depth and production, lower-cost effectors, or operational testing.
  4. 10m. Open questions with a "known-by" signal for each: FTM-43 result, a Block IB restart decision, the next DOT&E annual report, FY2027 appropriation for SM-6.
  5. 5m. Learning record.

Option B: enterprise-architecture view

  1. Fill the Sense, Share, Decide, Act, Assess, Sustain table for the naval and the joint or land implementation, with one assurance concern and one interface risk per row.
  2. Render it as a diagram (Mermaid in the note, or /drawio for a Draw.io file in C:\output\).
  3. Write which row is the binding constraint today and why.

Save the artifact to learning-records/ with today's date in the file name.

Retrieval check

Question

What did you ship today that proves working fluency in Raytheon SM-6?

Answer cues

A good artifact names events, not adjectives. It separates FTM-32 (live) from FTX-40 (simulated), cites DOT&E on operational effectiveness, states cost with its basis, and ends with dated signals to watch. If the brief could have been written without the sources, it does not count.

Exit criteria

  • Application artifact saved in learning-records/
  • Learning record written (what evidence shows you can do it?)
