Mission — Raytheon SM-6

Mission for one-day mastery of Raytheon SM-6.

Why

One focused day to reach working fluency in Raytheon SM-6 as a system: what it is, how the kill chain gives it reach, what the public test record proves, and where its limits sit. The spine is SM-6 assessment page, the first page of the Weapons section.

Learner

An analyst or architect who needs to discuss SM-6 accurately with defense program people and catch overclaims in briefs, vendor material, and press.

Success looks like

  • Can state SM-6's identity, missions, and kill-chain parts without notes
  • Can grade any SM-6 claim against the public test record and name what each event did not establish
  • Can teach the topic in 5 minutes, and in 2 minutes to an executive
  • Ships one evidence-graded brief or enterprise-architecture view (block 7)

Out of scope

  • Classified performance: range, engagement envelope, seeker performance, electronic protection, probability of kill
  • Countermeasure techniques, frequencies, timelines, firing doctrine, or anything that would help defeat a fielded weapon. The day stays at the spine page's level of broad countermeasure classes.
  • Multi-week integrated air and missile defense academy depth
  • Other weapons (each gets its own page in the Weapons section)

Constraints

  • Time box: ~350 focused minutes
  • Open sources only. Ground claims in RESOURCES / source-ledger

Falsifier

If you can recite specifications at the end of the day but cannot say what FTM-32 did not establish, the day failed.
